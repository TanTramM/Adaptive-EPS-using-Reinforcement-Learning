Caption:
Fig. X. Block diagram of the assist-map control loop.

Nomenclature (in the text of the paper):
Y   = T_s, steering torque (controlled variable)
U   = T_a, assist torque (manipulated variable)
D   = theta_1, driver steering angle (disturbance variable)
P   = T_a,cmd, assist-torque command (controller output)
Y_m = T_s,m, measured steering torque
v, v_m = vehicle speed and its measurement
Y_u, Y_d = parts of T_s due to the assist torque and to the driver angle
G_c = assist map M(v, T_s) with the lead-lag compensator C_map(s)
G_v = assist-torque limit +-T_a,max(v) and motor G_a(s)
G_p = response of T_s to T_a (negative: a larger assist torque lowers T_s, Eq. (15))
G_d = response of T_s to theta_1 (steering column)
G_m = torque and speed sensors

Text: The assist map has no set point; the reference is contained implicitly in the calibration of the map (dry road). The measured torque and speed are fed back to G_c, so the loop is closed through T_s.
