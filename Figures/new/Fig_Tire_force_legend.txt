Caption:
Fig. X. Top-view geometry of the front-tire steering angle, slip angle, and
lateral force.

Notation:
delta_f = theta_2/n_st   front-wheel steering angle
v_f                      velocity at the equivalent front tire
alpha_f                  angle from the velocity direction to the wheel plane
F_yf(alpha_f,mu)         front-axle lateral tire force in the road plane

Scope:
This figure defines only the road-plane quantities used in the front slip-angle
and lateral-force equations. It deliberately omits caster trail, pneumatic
trail, and the effective steering moment arm. Those quantities remain modeling
parameters in Eqs. (9)-(11), but their adopted dimensions have not been
validated for a specific vehicle and are therefore not presented as measured
geometry.
