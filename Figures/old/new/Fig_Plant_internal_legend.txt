Caption:
Fig. X. Internal signal flow of the nonlinear coupled C-EPS-vehicle plant.

Layout convention:
All external plant inputs enter through the left boundary, and all external
plant outputs leave through the right boundary. Each signal leaves its source
block from the right edge. The backward-running lines below the forward path
are physical feedback signals inside the plant, not external output ports.

Scope:
The dashed boundary contains only the steering column, tires, and 2-DOF
vehicle body. The assist controller, motor dynamics, torque limit, and
measurement models are outside this boundary.

Inputs:
theta_1                   prescribed driver steering-wheel angle
T_a                       assist torque applied to the lower column
v                         true longitudinal vehicle speed
mu                        tire-road friction coefficient

Outputs:
T_s                       true torsion-bar torque
theta_2_dot               lower-column angular velocity
a_y                       true vehicle lateral acceleration
gamma                     true vehicle yaw rate

Internal signals:
theta_2                   lower-column angle supplied to the tire kinematics
F_yf, F_yr                front and rear lateral tire forces
T_r                       road-reaction torque returned to the steering column
beta, gamma               vehicle states returned to the tire slip-angle model

Block relations:
The steering-column block represents Eqs. (1)-(2), the tire block represents
Eqs. (3)-(11), and the 2-DOF vehicle-body block represents Eqs. (12)-(14) of
System Modeling and Problem Formulation. The blocks are nonlinear state and
algebraic operators rather than transfer functions.
