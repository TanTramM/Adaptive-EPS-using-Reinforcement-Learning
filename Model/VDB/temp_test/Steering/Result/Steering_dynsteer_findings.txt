CAU HOI: co dung duoc khoi nay lam he lai DOC LAP (thay cot lai Lee cua Plant) de kiem ba bo dieu khien khong?

1. CONG
   Vao: TrqIn (mo-men NGUOI LAI dat len vo-lang), TrqLft, TrqRght (mo-men tu hai banh), VehSpd.
   Ra: Info (bus: StrgWhlAng, StrgWhlSpd, ShftAng, ShftSpd, AngLft, SpdLft, AngRght, SpdRght, TrqAst, PwrAst, PwrLoss, InstStrgRatio), AngLft, AngRght.

2. CAU TRUC BEN TRONG (doc tu day noi)
   Cot (vo-lang): J1 * ddot(theta_c) = TrqIn + TrqAst - b2 * dot(theta_c) - T          (J1 0.1, b2 1)
   Truc (thanh rang): J2 * ddot(theta_s) = T + TrqEq + ma sat - b3 * dot(theta_s)      (J2 0.01, b3 0.001)
   T = mo-men phan tu xoan (Backlash1: do cung k1 30 N.m/rad, can b1, tre beta_u, beta_l) = tuong ung T_s cua ta. KHONG co cong ra cho T.
   TrqEq = mo-men hai banh quy ve truc qua Jacobian Ackerman (TrqLft, TrqRght).
   TrqAst = bang tra (TrqIn cua nguoi lai, VehSpd) -> chan TrqLmt -> gioi han cong suat PwrLmt -> nhan Eta -> loc bac 1 (omega_c 200 rad/s). Bang dang tra theo mo-men NGUOI LAI (TrqMan = TrqIn), khong theo mo-men phan tu xoan.
   TrqAst cong vao TrqIn ngay o phia COT (J1), cung nut voi mo-men nguoi lai, o phia TREN phan tu xoan.

3. TRA LOI BA CAU HOI
   (1) Khoi co xuat T_s khong: KHONG. Chi tai tao gan dung duoc: T = TrqIn + TrqAst - b2*StrgWhlSpd - J1*d(StrgWhlSpd)/dt (can dao ham so), hoac k1*(StrgWhlAng - ShftAng) bo qua do tre.
   (2) Thay tro luc trong khoi bang mo-men motor ngoai: KHONG co cong. Tro luc cua khoi nam o phia cot, phia tren phan tu xoan; Plant cua ta: mo-men motor T_a o phia truc/pinion, SAU thanh xoan (plant.txt PT (5): T_s = T_r - T_a).
       Neu tat PwrAst va dua T_a cua ta vao TrqIn thi o xac lap T = tai tu hai banh, KHONG phu thuoc tro luc; mo-men can do khong con la T_s, nen vong hoi tiep mo-men cua ba bo dieu khien khong con y nghia nhu o Plant.
   (3) Dau vao cua khoi la MO-MEN nguoi lai, khong phai goc vo-lang; de ep goc theta1 nhu cach cham diem hien nay can sua ben trong.

4. KET LUAN
   Khong lap truc tiep duoc. Muon dung phai bo lien ket thu vien va sua ben trong (dua tro luc ve phia truc, xuat T, them dau vao goc); khi do la mo hinh cua ta, mat gia tri "khoi chuan".
   Khuyen nghi: BO khoi Dynamic Steering; giu cot lai Lee cua Plant.

5. KHOI KHAC TRONG THU VIEN CO THE LAM THAN VA LOP DOC LAP HON (chi liet ke ten, CHUA thu)
   Lop: Combined Slip Wheel 2DOF (Magic Formula, dang dung), Fiala Wheel 2DOF (mo hinh choi, khac Magic Formula), Dugoff Wheel 2DOF.
   Than: Vehicle Body 3DOF Single Track (dang dung), 3DOF Dual Track (hai vet: co chuyen tai khi cua), 6DOF.
   He treo: Independent Suspension (Double Wishbone, K and C, MacPherson, Mapped), Solid Axle..., Twist-Beam.
   Chua kiem: cach doc mo-men tu can (M_z) tu Fiala, cach tinh T_r tu hai vet, do kho ghep vao Plant.
